Who We Are

ATS RESUME is a team of HR professionals, career coaches, and industry experts dedicated to helping job seekers navigate the increasingly complex world of modern hiring processes.

We understand the challenges of today's job market, where Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) and AI-powered screening tools can make it difficult for even the most qualified candidates to get noticed.

Our mission is to level the playing field by providing professional resume writing services that not only showcase your skills and experience but are also optimized to pass through digital screening systems and impress human recruiters.

Our Story

ATS RESUME was founded in 2018 by a group of HR professionals who saw firsthand how qualified candidates were being filtered out by automated systems before their applications ever reached human eyes.

After years of working in corporate recruitment, our founders recognized a growing disconnect between job seekers and employers. They witnessed talented professionals struggling to navigate the increasingly digital hiring landscape, while employers were missing out on great candidates due to flawed screening processes.

What began as a small consulting practice has grown into a comprehensive resume writing service with a team of specialists from various industries. Our growth has been driven by our commitment to staying ahead of hiring trends and technologies, and our passion for helping people advance their careers.

Today, we've helped thousands of professionals at all career stages land interviews and secure positions at their dream companies. We continue to evolve our services to address the changing needs of job seekers in an increasingly competitive and technology-driven job market.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Integrity

We believe in honest representation of your skills and experience. We'll help you present your best self without misrepresentation or exaggeration.

Expertise

Our team stays at the forefront of hiring trends, ATS technologies, and industry-specific requirements to provide you with the most current and effective resume strategies.

Personalization

We reject one-size-fits-all approaches. Every resume we create is tailored to your unique experience, skills, and career goals.

Empowerment

Beyond providing a service, we aim to educate and empower you with knowledge about effective job search strategies and personal branding.

Results-Driven

We measure our success by your success. Our ultimate goal is to help you land interviews and secure positions that advance your career.

Accessibility

We strive to make professional resume services accessible to job seekers at all career levels, from entry-level to executive positions.
