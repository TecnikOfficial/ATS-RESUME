Frequently Asked Questions

Home › FAQ

Find answers to the most common questions about our resume writing services. If you don't see your question here, please contact us.

How does your resume writing process work?

Our process begins with a detailed questionnaire and consultation to understand your career goals and experience. Then, our HR professionals craft a custom resume tailored to your specific needs. You'll receive a draft for review, and we'll make revisions based on your feedback before delivering the final documents.

What makes your resumes better than AI-generated ones?

AI tools lack the human understanding of career nuances, industry-specific knowledge, and the strategic positioning that our HR professionals bring. Our resumes are crafted with an understanding of what recruiters actually look for, how hiring decisions are made, and how to present your unique value proposition effectively.

How long does it take to get my resume?

Delivery times vary by package: Essential (5-7 days), Professional (3-5 days), and Executive (2-3 days). These timeframes begin after we receive your completed questionnaire and any additional information we need.

What if I'm not satisfied with my resume?

We offer revision rounds with each package to ensure your satisfaction. If you're still not getting results after 60 days of using your new resume, we'll review and revise it at no additional cost under our satisfaction guarantee.

What format will I receive my resume in?

You'll receive your resume in multiple formats: Word (.docx) for easy editing, PDF for consistent formatting when sharing, and a plain text version optimized for online application systems.

Do you write resumes for all industries?

Yes, our team has experience writing resumes for professionals across diverse industries including technology, healthcare, finance, education, manufacturing, creative fields, and more. We match you with a writer who has relevant industry knowledge.

What is ATS optimization and why is it important?

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) are software used by employers to scan and rank resumes before a human sees them. Our ATS optimization ensures your resume contains the right keywords, formatting, and structure to pass through these systems successfully, increasing your chances of getting an interview.

Can I see samples of your work?

Due to client confidentiality, we don't share actual client resumes. However, we can provide anonymized samples that demonstrate our writing style, formatting, and overall approach. Please contact us to request samples relevant to your industry.
